About

IAM operations with a governance mindset.

I currently support SailPoint operations and work closely with application owners, cybersecurity, risk, and compliance teams. My background also includes RSA Identity Governance & Lifecycle and Saviynt.

I focus on making IAM processes controlled, traceable, and practical. That means not only granting access, but understanding why access exists, who approved it, how it was provisioned, how it is reviewed, and how quickly it is removed when it is no longer appropriate.
